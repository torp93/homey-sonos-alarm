Wake up to your own music. Set the alarms on your Sonos speakers from Homey, and let them start your morning with a radio station, a playlist or the built-in chime. The alarms live in the speakers themselves, so they keep going off even when Homey is switched off.

Everything happens on your own network, without a Sonos account or a cloud connection, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to renew. Alarms you already made in the Sonos app show up straight away, and anything you change here appears there just as quickly.
